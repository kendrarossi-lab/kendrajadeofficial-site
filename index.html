Speak No Evil, the novel

Wren Walter kept quiet for twenty years. Three masked figures named SEE, HEAR and SPEAK made sure of it.

Now she is dead at twenty-six, waking on a pale wooden floor in an endless grey, and the monkeys came with her. Above her: a choir, and a light the color of her grandmother's lamp. Below her: a red glow that flares every time she thinks of the man who hurt her.

Her grandmother is waiting to take her home. Wren wants to go down instead. She wants to watch him burn.

Adapted from the rock opera of the same name, Speak No Evil is a literary afterlife novel about what silence costs a family, and what one voice can do.

Content note: this novel deals with child sexual abuse, sex trafficking and suicide. None is depicted graphically. Resources for readers are included at the back of the book.

About Kendra Jade

Kendra Jade is a writer and the creator of Speak No Evil, a rock opera for which she wrote the book, music and lyrics. This novel is adapted from that show and carries her lyrics throughout. She is also the founder of Krossi Culture, the brand behind her planners and the imprint that publishes this book.
